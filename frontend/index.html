Points are for fun and community streaks — they have no monetary value. AgentStreak never asks for approvals and never moves your funds; a check-in is a single small transaction you confirm yourself.

How it works

  • Only Agent ID holders can check in — your wallet is checked on-chain before the button unlocks.
  • One check-in per day (UTC). Check in on consecutive days to grow your streak; miss a day and the streak restarts at 1.
  • Each check-in earns 10 points plus a streak bonus of up to 20 points.
  • Levels: Starter at 1 check-in, Regular at 7, Committed at 14, Core Agent at 30.
  • No Agent ID yet? Agent IDs are claimed inside the Binance App: Wallet > homepage banner > Agent ID.
